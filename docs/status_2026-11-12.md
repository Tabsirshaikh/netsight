## Honest headline results (for pitches)

1. On surveys it never saw, the detector averages AP50 0.42; papers' 75-99% come from random
   splits (our pipeline scores 0.72 that way and 0.21 on unseen survey years).
2. A hand-set detection-probability grid (what other projects use) claims 0.60-0.95; measured Pd
   is 0.24-0.59: every hand-set value is above the measured 95% interval.
3. Calibrated probabilities are honest on held-out surveys (ECE 0.013-0.062 per set).
4. The applicability gate catches sensor/geometry shifts (all Contact and mine blocks flagged)
   but not recall dips within one sensor.
5. Negative results are kept: evidence fusion, parametric reference targets, zoom augmentation
   (v0.3) and the shipwreck dataset (v0.5) all made things worse and were rejected.

## Known gaps

- No real buyer interviews yet; demand still unproven.
- On 1 m NOAA mosaics the detector found 0 of 7 confirmed objects (too coarse for this model).
- GSF/KMALL readers and the UXO mode are untested on real vendor data / by a UXO expert.
- Withdrawal on a truly new sensor family cannot be tested with current public data.
