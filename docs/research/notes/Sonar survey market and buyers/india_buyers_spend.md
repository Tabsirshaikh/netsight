# India buyers of underwater / hydrographic / sonar survey work: who buys, how much, how (as of Nov 2026)

Method and verification legend (read first)
- "OPENED" = I fetched the page/PDF and read the stated fact. "SEARCH-SUMMARY" = the fact comes only from a web-search result summary; I could not open the page (HTTP 403, timeout, or content unreadable). Treat SEARCH-SUMMARY items as leads to be confirmed.
- Tender portals: GeM, CPPP/eprocure.gov.in and Tender Tiger/Tender Detail could not be queried directly (they need interactive/session access). Direct fetches failed: iwai.nic.in (connection refused), ndrf.gov.in (certificate error), vocport.gov.in PDF links (returned an HTML page, not the PDF), hydro-international.com, pib.gov.in, oceansciencetechnology.com, unmannedsystemstechnology.com (HTTP 403), idex.gov.in (timeout / unreadable). Proxies used instead: port-hosted tender PDFs, tender aggregators (TenderShark, Tender18, Infralens), trade press, parliamentary/IHO documents.
- No full-year tender census was possible. Everything below is a sample of individual tenders, not a count of market frequency.

## 1. Ports (major ports, state boards, private ports): do they tender hydrographic / side-scan / wreck surveys, at what value?

### Takeaway
Ports do tender hydrographic work, but the verified examples are small (about Rs 33 lakh over three years at New Mangalore; about Rs 50 lakh at V.O. Chidambaranar) and are mostly bathymetry for dredging control, not side-scan, wreck or obstruction search. I found no port tender for a wreck/obstruction survey and no award values for JNPA, Chennai, Kochi, Visakhapatnam, Paradip, Deendayal, Mormugao, Vizhinjam, GMB or Adani ports.

### Cited Findings
- New Mangalore Port Authority, NIT CIVIL/CE(C)/EE(C)/95/2022-23 (dated 20-07-2023): tender for "witnessing and certifying the pre and post hydrographic survey work as a third party agency" for maintenance dredging, FY2023-24 to 2025-26 (3 years). Estimated amount Rs 32,87,847 (about Rs 33 lakh total, roughly Rs 11 lakh/year); EMD Rs 77,600; tender fee Rs 560; each year max 32 days pre-survey and 32 days post-survey; published on the port site and CPP portal (eprocure.gov.in). OPENED — [NMPA tender PDF](https://newmangaloreport.gov.in/index.php/sites/default/files/2023-07/HydrographicSurveyTD.pdf)
- Same NMPA tender, scope: verify calibration of survey equipment, supervise/approve pre- and post-dredge surveys, approve and certify bathymetry charts, i.e. an independent auditor of the dredging contractor's own survey. OPENED — [NMPA tender PDF](https://newmangaloreport.gov.in/index.php/sites/default/files/2023-07/HydrographicSurveyTD.pdf)
- Same NMPA tender, eligibility: experience of 3 similar works of at least Rs 13.20 lakh each, or 2 of at least Rs 16.45 lakh, or 1 of at least Rs 26.35 lakh within 7 years; "similar works" = carrying out or witnessing/certifying hydrographic survey; average turnover over 3 years at least Rs 9.90 lakh. OPENED — [NMPA tender PDF](https://newmangaloreport.gov.in/index.php/sites/default/files/2023-07/HydrographicSurveyTD.pdf)
- V.O. Chidambaranar Port (Tuticorin), NIT 96, Engg(Civil), Sept 2025: bathymetry survey of inner harbour basin and approach channel; estimate Rs 50,16,500, EMD Rs 1,00,330; documents downloadable 26.09.2025 to 16.10.2025; scope reportedly includes a DGPS side-scan sonar and survey boat to monitor continuous dredging. SEARCH-SUMMARY only (the PDF link returned an HTML page to my fetch) — [VOC Port NIT 96](https://www.vocport.gov.in/VOCPORT_HINDI/port/UserInterface/DailyVessel/Engg(Civil)_20250926184439_NIT_96.pdf)
- VOC Port also has an earlier technical-bid file dated Sept 2024 and a 2025 technical-bid file for the same survey tender series (titles only seen in search results; contents not read). SEARCH-SUMMARY — [VOC 2024 technical bid](https://vocport.gov.in/VOCPORT_HINDI/port/UserInterface/DailyVessel/Engg(Civil)_20240919131052_Technicalbid_69.pdf), [VOC 2025 technical bid](https://www.vocport.gov.in/VOCPORT_HINDI/port/UserInterface/DailyVessel/Engg(Civil)_20250926184439_Technicalbid_12.pdf)
- Paradip Port Authority listing: "Providing Assistance for Monitoring Maintenance Dredging Operation and Assisting Hydrographic Survey and other Miscellaneous works of Hydrographic Survey Division, Marine Department" for 3 years; no value seen. This indicates that at least one major port runs an in-house Hydrographic Survey Division and hires manpower assistance. - Deendayal Port (Kandla): an expression of interest (April 2024) for a hydrographic survey of the navigational channel and berth pockets at Ghogha, 1:2000 scale charts, 210 kHz; no estimated cost shown. SEARCH-SUMMARY only (the PDF fetch returned only the port name) — [Deendayal EOI-3](https://www.deendayalport.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/EOI-3.pdf)

- Port survey track record of one private consultancy (Mumbai, Bharuch, Krishnapatnam, Bhavnagar, Ratnagiri) exists, but it is a personal profile, not a tender or award. SEARCH-SUMMARY — [Bold profile](https://in.bold.pro/my/yogesh-kumarsingh)

### Inferences
- The only fully verified port contract is a ~Rs 11 lakh/year third-party witnessing job, i.e. a small, bathymetry-centred, dredging-control purchase with low eligibility thresholds (turnover Rs 9.9 lakh). This is the right order of magnitude for a single port's recurring survey spend, not a large budget line.
- The "third-party certification of the dredger's survey" model is the closest real analogue to a QA/review product, but the certification is of bathymetry charts, not side-scan target interpretation.
- Side-scan sonar appears (if the VOC summary is right) as an equipment line inside a dredging-monitoring survey, not as a stand-alone wreck/obstruction search procurement.
- The earlier claim that survey is under 1% of a dredging project is not contradicted or confirmed here: I did not find the NMPA maintenance dredging contract value to compute a ratio.

### Gaps
- No GeM or CPPP search was possible, so no frequency count across 12 major ports; no awards/winners for any port.
- No tender found for wreck survey, obstruction survey, or side-scan-only work at any port; no tender found for Vizhinjam, JNPA, Chennai, Kochi, Mormugao, Gujarat Maritime Board, or Adani ports.
- VOC and Deendayal documents could not be opened; their scope and values are search-summary only.

## 2. IWAI and Jal Marg Vikas

### Takeaway
IWAI is a recurring buyer of outsourced hydrographic work (detailed hydrographic surveys and fortnightly/monthly thalweg surveys across many waterways), run as open tenders to private survey firms, but I could not retrieve any contract value.

### Cited Findings
- IWAI open tender IWAI-15017/29/2024-Hy: detailed hydrographic survey of National Waterway 2 (Neamatighat to Dibrugarh, Assam). SEARCH-SUMMARY — [IWAI/aggregator result set](https://tendershark.com/authority/inland-waterways-authority-of-india-tenders/active)
- IWAI open tenders (Tender ID 2025_IWAI_846191, ref. NW MH GJ Goa KN/MLTS/2024-25) for monthly longitudinal bathymetric (thalweg) survey in Maharashtra (Schedule A), Gujarat (B), Goa (C), Karnataka (D). SEARCH-SUMMARY; cost field not visible. Thalweg survey = single-lane longitudinal survey along the deepest channel, fortnightly/monthly. — [Geospatial World overview](https://geospatialworld.net/article/surveying-and-mapping-of-national-waterways)
- A 2025 report says IWAI conducts about 10,000 km of longitudinal survey each month to assess Least Available Depth; a 2026 explainer says regular thalweg surveys cover 22 National Waterways (secondary source, treat with caution). SEARCH-SUMMARY — [Shankar IAS explainer](https://www.shankariasparliament.com/current-affairs/navigating-indias-waterways)
- The one IWAI listing I could open (TenderShark, active list) showed no survey tender; the only item near the topic was a DPR consultancy for RCC community jetties on NW-30/32/50/57/95, estimated Rs 5.56 crore, closing 03 Nov 2026 (not a survey tender; do not use as survey value). OPENED — [TenderShark IWAI](https://tendershark.com/authority/inland-waterways-authority-of-india-tenders/active)
- Older: a Lok Sabha reply said a detailed hydrographic survey of NW-4 (Vijayawada-Wazirabad) was awarded to a Delhi firm in January 2015. SEARCH-SUMMARY — [Lok Sabha document](https://eparlib.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/659522/1/14189.pdf)
- Jal Marg Vikas Project (World Bank-funded, NW-1) recent awards found are fairway maintenance/dredging (for example a Rs 147 crore maintenance contract won by KMEW), not survey. SEARCH-SUMMARY — [Maritime Gateway](https://www.maritimegateway.com/kmew-obtains-rs-147-crore-waterway-maintenance-contract-from-iwai)

### Inferences
- IWAI survey work is river bathymetry (echo-sounder/thalweg), repeated monthly across many waterways, which is a recurring-volume buyer but with river-depth deliverables rather than side-scan target detection.

### Gaps
- No IWAI survey contract values, winners, or annual survey budget found. IWAI site unreachable from my environment.

## 3. Indian Navy / National Hydrographic Office (INHD): outsourcing, Sandhayak cost, data access

### Takeaway
The Navy surveys in-house with its own ships; the Sandhayak programme is about Rs 2,435 crore for four ships, and NHO says its customers include "Commercial", but I found no evidence that NHO outsources survey work or a published policy for civil agencies buying its data.

### Cited Findings
- INHD (with NHO Dehradun) is the national chart authority; it has "six modern ocean-going survey ships and one twin hulled catamaran survey vessel"; its stated customers are Armed Forces, Coast Guard, Govt. Depts and "Commercial"; data collection uses AUVs (two) and multibeam; "technology absorption for data collection and processing is being undertaken with in-house skills and techniques". OPENED (downloaded and text-extracted) — [INHD national report to SAIHC-19 (2023)](https://iho.int/uploads/user/Inter-Regional%20Coordination/RHC/SAIHC/SAIHC19/SAIHC19-7.11_2023_India%20National%20Report.pdf)
- Sandhayak class (4 large survey vessels): contract Rs 2,435.15 crore awarded by MoD to GRSE on 30 Oct 2018; displacement ~3,300 t; delivered/commissioned: Sandhayak (commissioned 3 Feb 2024, Visakhapatnam), Nirdeshak (18 Dec 2024), Ikshak (6 Nov 2025, Kochi), Sanshodhak (delivered 30 Mar 2026, commissioned 21 Jun 2026). Primary role: oceanographic data "for defence". OPENED — [Wikipedia, Sandhayak-class (2023)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandhayak-class_survey_vessel_(2023)) (secondary source; the Rs 2,435 crore figure is also cited in trade press)
- Sandhayak sensor fit (4 HydroSweep DS deep-water, 8 SeaBat T-series, 36 DESO 5 echo sounders) supplied by Teledyne with PAN India Consultants as service partner; NHO awarded two Teledyne RESON multibeam systems in 2021, with later orders for more. SEARCH-SUMMARY (counts vary by source) — [Oceannews](https://oceannews.com/?p=62626), [Teledyne](https://www.teledynemarine.com/indian-naval-ship-ins-sandhayak-commissioned-in-visakhapatnam-with-state-of-the-art-sonar-systems)
- A national report to the IHO (older edition) says INHD also "carries out surveys for the private sector"; one IHO listing shows outsourcing policy as "Nil" (snippet only). SEARCH-SUMMARY, unverified — [IHO NIOHC report index](https://legacy.iho.int/mtg_docs/rhc/NIOHC/NIOHC13/NIOHC%2013-6-1-15%20-%20India%20National%20Report.pdf)

### Inferences
- The Navy is a hardware/sensor buyer (multibeam, AUVs) and an in-house analyst. With 4 new survey ships and in-house processing, it is an unlikely buyer of outsourced sonar-review services, though it could be a collaborator or iDEX-route customer.
- The ships' published sensor lists feature multibeam and echo sounders; side-scan is not listed on the Wikipedia page (the 2013 Kongsberg side-scan package was for a different (GSI) vessel).

### Gaps
- No NHO outsourcing contracts, no data-sale price list or policy for civil agencies, no NHO budget figure found.

Research bodies and ONGC do buy survey services and equipment (including outsourcing coastal bathymetry to Fugro and ONGC multi-year survey contracts), but amounts are in the USD millions only for large oil/gas and national coastal mapping work; disaster forces buy ROVs/imaging sonar in small unit numbers, and none of this is for AI review of side-scan.

### Takeaway

Most other named firms (Geoscience, Ocean Data Services, Deep Sea Technologies, Seabed Surveys, Aquasurvey, Geomarine) returned no public customer/revenue data in my searches.

### Gaps

## 6. Offshore wind and subsea cables

### Takeaway
Offshore wind is stalled: auctions were cancelled and NIWE's survey was reported complete in March 2026, so there is no active contracted UXO/debris survey market in India yet. Cable landings are happening but I found no published marine route-survey contracts.

### Cited Findings
- SECI cancelled two offshore wind tenders totalling 4.5 GW in August 2025 (4 GW Tamil Nadu seabed lease and 500 MW Gujarat); Rs 7,453 crore VGF approved for the first 1 GW (500 MW each in Tamil Nadu and Gujarat); the reworked 1 GW tender is "ready" but the ministry is undecided on launching; NIWE's survey is "over" and report submitted (article of 13 March 2026). OPENED — [Down To Earth](https://www.downtoearth.org.in/energy/india-reconsiders-1-gw-offshore-wind-tender-after-earlier-auctions-fail-to-attract-bidders-official-tells-dte)
- NIWE floated a tender (3 Nov 2023, bids due 28 Nov 2023) for offshore geophysical and geotechnical investigations in Gulf of Mannar sub-zone 1, Tamil Nadu; the visible text mentions neither side-scan, UXO nor boulders, no value, no winner. OPENED (lead paragraph only) — [Mercom](https://mercomindia.com/niwe-floats-tender-for-offshore-tamil-nadu)
- NIWE tendered geotechnical investigation in the Gulf of Khambhat (Gujarat) in 2018 and reinvited bids in Dec 2018 for 1,000 MW; no awardee found. SEARCH-SUMMARY — [Offshorewind.biz](https://www.offshorewind.biz/2018/09/11/niwe-extends-gujarat-geotechnical-survey-tender-deadline), [Renewable Watch](https://renewablewatch.in/2018/12/27/niwe-reinvites-bids-conducting-geotechnical-investigation-1000-mw-offshore-wind-projects/)
- Airtel landed SEA-ME-WE 6 in Mumbai (30 Dec 2024) and Chennai (Feb 2025); Meta's Waterworth reportedly chose Sify as India landing partner (about $5 million, unconfirmed), landings Mumbai and Visakhapatnam; MIST (Singapore-Chennai-Mumbai) pegged around USD 400 million. SEARCH-SUMMARY — [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/amp/story/news/business/airtel-completes-landing-of-sea-me-we-6-submarine-cable-in-chennai-823354), [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/sify-technologies-to-connect-india-to-metas-waterworth-project)

### Inferences
- In India, developers' site surveys come after leasing/auction; with auctions cancelled, offshore wind site-survey spend is deferred (and the early geophysical work was done by NIWE, a government body).
- Cable route/landing surveys are commissioned by cable consortia, usually from international survey firms, so Indian buyer reach would be indirect.

### Gaps
- No evidence of UXO/boulder/debris survey scope in any Indian offshore wind document; no cable route-survey contractor names for Indian waters.

## 7. Disaster response and search-and-recovery (incl. Shirur / Gangavali 2024)

### Takeaway
Real demand is visible in the 2024 Shirur search, where the Navy used sonar and a Rs 96 lakh dredger was proposed, but no dedicated sonar-search budget line or contract was found; the market is episodic and hardware-led.

### Cited Findings
- Shirur (Ankola, Uttara Kannada) landslide, July 2024: the Uttara Kannada District Collector reportedly wrote that bringing in a dredger would cost Rs 96 lakh; Navy sonar detected a strong signal for a large object; truck belonging to driver Arjun found in Gangavali in late July; search paused after 13 days then resumed in August with diver Eshwar Malpe; strong currents hindered diving. SEARCH-SUMMARY — [Kerala Kaumudi](https://keralakaumudi.com/en/news/news.php?id=1371983), [Onmanorama](https://onmanorama.com/news/kerala/2024/07/24/ankola-shirur-landslide-truck-located-arjun-search-karnataka.html), [Udayavani](https://english.udayavani.com/article/shirur-landslide-eshwar-malpe-and-team-joins-search-for-missing-truck-driver-and-others/1677281)
- Disaster-response sonar: after 2024 flood season "officials asked for underwater imaging"; Banergy ordered one Cerulean sonar unit to test; no government agency, price or contract named. OPENED — [Oceannews, 12 June 2026](https://oceannews.com/?p=115867)
- NDRF GeM EOI for ROV with sonar and Gandhinagar municipal ROV tender (see section 4). 

### Inferences
- Total documented spend in this segment is on the order of tens of lakhs of rupees per event (dredger estimate) or ROV unit purchases; it is episodic.

### Gaps
- No sonar-specific cost for the Shirur search; Wayanad not covered; no state police/NDRF sonar-service contracts found.

## 8. Government funding channels (Startup India, DST/MoES, iDEX, TDB, SAMRIDH, Deep Ocean Mission) and SIH26057

### Takeaway
The Deep Ocean Mission is a large ocean programme (Rs 4,077 crore over five years) but I found no open funding call for AI underwater detection; iDEX has funded sonar/AI underwater challenges (and Planys Ark claims three iDEX wins). SIH26057 shows no evidence of linkage to a real procurement.

### Cited Findings
- Deep Ocean Mission: estimated Rs 4,077 crore over five years (2021-2026); phase 1 (2021-24) Rs 2,823.4 crore; early allocations Rs 150 crore (2021-22) and Rs 650 crore (2022-23); MoES is the nodal ministry; includes a research vessel and the Matsya 6000 submersible. SEARCH-SUMMARY (PIB page returned HTTP 403) — [PIB release](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1797250), [MoES copy](https://moes.gov.in/sites/default/files/PIB1797250.pdf)
- iDEX: Indian Navy DISC-7 results (28 Sept 2022) list winners for a 3D forward-looking sonar challenge and AI adaptive noise cancellation for sonars; other listed challenges cover underwater photography noise cancellation with AI and AUV mine countermeasures. SEARCH-SUMMARY — [iDEX DISC-7 result](https://idex.gov.in/uploads/notice/indian%20navy%20result%20disc%207%20Updated%20as%20of%2029th%20Sep_0.pdf), [iDEX challenge 165](https://idex.gov.in/challenges-cpt/165)
- Planys Ark says it is "backed by three iDEX challenge wins". OPENED — [ITWire](https://itwire.com/it-industry-news/strategy/planys-raises-%E2%82%B9100-crore-to-accelerate-global-expansion-and-launch-defence-arm,-planys-ark.html)
- NIOT held a 2-day Underwater Robotics Workshop (UWR-2026, about 300 delegates) in Chennai in June 2026; not a grant call. SEARCH-SUMMARY — [UNI](https://test.uniindia.com/2-day-niot-national-underwater-robotics-workshop-uwr-2026-begins/south/news/3862183.html)
- SIH26057: I could not find the official problem statement or any procurement link; searches returned only academic side-scan papers. NOT FOUND.

### Inferences
