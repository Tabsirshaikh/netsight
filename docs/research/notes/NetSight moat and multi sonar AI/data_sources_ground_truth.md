# Sonar Data Sources and Verified Ground Truth for NetSight (as of Nov 2026)

Verification legend used throughout: **[V]** = the licence or contents were read directly on the hosting page in this session. **[S]** = known only from a search snippet, a secondary summary or a survey table, not from the host page. **[U]** = a lead that was not checked (listed only in Gaps).

## 1. Public labelled datasets by sensor (size, labels, licence, URL)

### Takeaway
About 25 public sonar datasets exist across SSS, FLS, SAS, MBES and DIDSON. Almost all are small: a few hundred to about 10k images, apart from the 434k-patch unlabelled BenthiCat set and the 74k-frame UXO set. Licences that clearly allow commercial use (CC BY 4.0, BSD-3, CC0) were confirmed only for a handful: the Catalan seafloor-sediment SSS set, SubPipe, the Bath mannequin SSS set, the DFKI UXO set and NOAA survey products. Most Chinese-origin sets (SCTD, KLSG, NKSID, UATD) have no dataset licence that I could confirm. No public dataset has ghost-net labels. No public dataset links each label to a later physical verification, except controlled-placement sets (UXO tank, mannequin, UATD lake targets), where ground truth is known by construction.

**Forward-looking / imaging sonar (FLS)**

Figshare 21331143. I found no data licence (the arXiv licence covers only the paper) [S] — [PMC9715547](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9715547/); [Gomes survey ref 43](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.03353)
- Marine Debris FLS datasets (Valdenegro-Toro et al., DFKI): the 2021 release has 1,868 ARIS Explorer 3000 images with 11 classes plus background for segmentation (best baseline UNet-ResNet34 at 0.7481 mIoU). The 2025 extension adds watertank, turntable and flooded-quarry settings, on Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.15101686 and GitHub mvaldenegro/marine-debris-fls-datasets. - NKSID (github.com/Jorwnpay/NK-Sonar-Image-Dataset; 2,617 FLS images, open-set recognition) and the FLS Detection dataset (github.com/XingYZhu/...; 3,752 images of victim/boat/plane). Licences not checked [S] — [Gomes survey](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.03353)
- SEE (Synthetic Enclosed Echoes, 2025): HoloOcean simulations paired with real BlueView P900 acquisitions, intended for 3D reconstruction. Built specifically to measure the sim-real gap [S] — [alphaxiv 2505.15465](https://www.alphaxiv.org/overview/2505.15465)

**Multibeam / water column**
- Aurora (NOC, IJRR 2022): AUV multi-sensor set with MBES (81 km) and SSS (15 h), open access on IEEE DataPort. MBES-SLAM (Krasnosky et al. 2022) has high-precision navigation ground truth. MSM95 and MSM30 are Kongsberg EM122 cruise datasets on PANGAEA. A UK Environment Agency MBES set is on data.gov.uk [S] — [Gomes survey refs 48–50, 55](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.03353)

### Inferences
- For calibration, the useful public "verified-real" positives come from three groups: (a) controlled placements (DFKI UXO, Bath mannequin, UATD lake targets, in-air SAS, Marine Debris watertank/quarry); (b) known archaeological wrecks (AI4Shipwrecks, Thunder Bay sanctuary); (c) engineered infrastructure (SubPipe pipeline, Marine-PULSE). None of them provides **verified false positives** (contacts that turned out to be nothing). That is the missing half of a reliability curve.
- For a commercial startup, the safe-to-use set today is the Catalan BenthiCat-style pretraining set (CC BY), SubPipe (CC BY), the Bath mannequin set (CC BY), DFKI UXO (BSD-3) and NOAA products (CC0). Everything else needs a licence check or a direct permission email first.
- SASSED's own statement that its labels are not seafloor-verified is a useful warning: most "labels" in public sonar datasets are expert interpretations, not ground truth.

### Gaps
- I could not read the licences for AI4Shipwrecks (Deep Blue 403), KLSG, SCTD/SCTD 3.0, NKSID, UATD, Marine Debris FLS, SASSED or Marine-PULSE. Check each LICENSE file or repository licence field. BenthiCat as a named dataset and the "Sonar ATR challenge" were not confirmed by name in this session.
- No NSWC/CMRE public SAS mine-countermeasure ATR benchmark beyond SASSED was found. CMRE MUSCLE data is not known to be public [U].
- No public water-column (WCD) object-detection dataset was found.

## 2. Open government survey archives (raw SSS/backscatter; is contact disposition recorded?)

### Takeaway
NOAA is by far the most open source. Per-survey NCEI folders publish BAG bathymetry, SSS mosaics (GeoTIFF), backscatter (MBAB) and Descriptive Reports (DRs) under CC0. The DRs and associated contact lists record how contacts were investigated and dispositioned. NOAA's Wrecks and Obstructions database (about 13,000 wrecks and 6,000 obstructions) is downloadable, but disproved or salvaged items are removed from AWOIS, which loses the negatives. Raw XTF is not in the public per-survey folders I checked, though USGS republishes some NOAA surveys with raw Klein XTF and contact lists. I could not confirm the UK Marine Data Exchange licence terms.

### Cited Findings
- NOAA NOS survey H13684 (2023): the data.gov record lists BAG (1 m MLLW and ellipsoid), **SSS mosaic GeoTIFFs (900 kHz, 1 m)** and the Descriptive Report (PDF+XML). **Licence CC0 1.0** [V]. The NCEI directory has subfolders BAG/, Bottom_Samples/, DR/, GeoImagePDF/, MBAB/ (multibeam backscatter) and TIFF/. It has **no raw XTF/GSF and no feature-file folder** [V] — [data.gov H13684](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/h13684-nos-hydrographic-survey-2023-04-29); [NCEI H13684 directory](https://data.ngdc.noaa.gov/platforms/ocean/nos/coast/H12001-H14000/H13684/)
- NCEI says SSS mosaics are available only for recent high-resolution NOS surveys. Data are "not suitable for navigation" — [NCEI hydro page (archived)](https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20170120084443/https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/mgg/bathymetry/hydro.html); [data.gov H12525](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/h12525-nos-hydrographic-survey-2013-06-131)
- Descriptive Reports describe how contacts were selected and dismissed. For example, F00459 notes that "many contacts were dismissed". H-survey DRs say a complete list of SSS contacts is kept in a separate volume. The HSSD 2017 summary says dangerous wrecks and obstructions go into the Final Feature File with investigation-requirement attribution on disprovals [S; HSSD via Scribd copy] — [F00459 DR](https://data.ngdc.noaa.gov/platforms/ocean/nos/coast/F00001-F02000/F00459/DR/F00459.pdf); [H11441 DR via USGS](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2009/1231/pdf_files/h11441_dr.pdf); [HSSD 2017 copy](https://www.scribd.com/document/544744827/hssd-2017)
- USGS Open-File Reports republish NOAA H-survey SSS for New England (Klein 5000/3000, XTF) with **contact listings**, e.g. OFR 2008-1174 (data and contacts pages), OFR 2006-1199 contacts, OFR 2014-1018 (H12296 1 m SSS mosaic) — [USGS OFR 2008-1174 data](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2008/1174/html/data.html); [OFR 2008-1174 contacts](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2008/1174/html/contacts.html); [OFR 2006-1199 contacts](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2006/1199/html/contacts.html); [OFR 2014-1018 SSS](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2014/1018/data/sidescan/h12296_1msss_utm19.html)
- USGS CMGDS dataset "2015-315-FA_xtf.zip" contains **unprocessed Klein SonarPro XTF** raw SSS. The Marine-Geo Digital Library hosts raw EdgeTech towed SSS XTF from RV Nathaniel B. Palmer cruises (e.g. NBP0505) [S] — [USGS CMGDS 38005](https://cmgds.er.usgs.gov/services/cmgds-dataset.php?id=38005); [MGDS 6159](https://www.marine-geo.org/tools/datasets/6159)
- NOAA OCS Wrecks and Obstructions database combines AWOIS and ENC wrecks: about 13,000 wreck features and 6,000 obstructions, downloadable as KML/XLS plus ArcGIS REST/WMS, with "Access Constraints: None". AWOIS stopped being updated in 2016, and **salvaged or disproved wrecks are not included in AWOIS** — [NOAA InPort 39988](https://fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/39988); [NOAA chart updates post](https://nauticalcharts.noaa.gov/updates/?p=947); [ArcGIS service](https://gis.charttools.noaa.gov/arcgis/rest/services/public_wrecks/Wrecks_And_Obstructions/MapServer/info/iteminfo); [AWOIS guide 2013](https://www.NauticalCharts.NOAA.gov/data/docs/wrecks-and-obstructions/awois-users-guide-2013.pdf)
- UK Marine Data Exchange (The Crown Estate) holds wind, tidal, wave, aggregates and research surveys with categories including Geophysical, Geotechnical and Engineering Design. Each listing has dataset and report counts. Login/sign-up is required. The terms of use were not visible [V for landing page] — [Marine Data Exchange](https://www.marinedataexchange.co.uk/)
- The Crown Estate says data arrive via a lease "data clause", and that over 60% of survey data from offshore renewables in England, Wales and NI is freely available via MDE. The public side shows data "no longer restricted" (2015 policy). Some other Crown Estate datasets carry a bespoke licence limited to "internal business use" [S] — [IMDIS 2018 paper](https://imdis.seadatanet.org/content/download/122262/file/S1P032_IMDIS2018.pdf); [NRW metadata example](https://metadata.naturalresources.wales/geonetwork/srv/api/records/EXT_DS121680)

### Inferences
- The best openly licensed source of real SSS with contact disposition is NOAA H-surveys: CC0 SSS mosaics, plus DR/contact lists, plus charted features, plus AWOIS/ENC wreck positions. Building a contact-to-outcome table from DRs means parsing PDFs. Negatives ("disproved") have to be recovered from DRs and feature files, not from AWOIS.
- MBAB backscatter in NCEI folders gives a free multibeam-backscatter modality on the same seabed as the SSS mosaic. That is useful for multi-sensor Pd per cell.

### Gaps
- I did not verify whether NCEI archives raw SSS XTF per H-survey in a separate path or on request (contact hsd.inquiries@noaa.gov), or where the Final Feature File (.000/.xml) is published.
- Not researched in this session: UKHO wrecks database licence (UKHO copyright site exists: [copyright.ukho.gov.uk](https://copyright.ukho.gov.uk/datasets.aspx)), Rijkswaterstaat/Noordzeeloket, BSH, MAREANO, AusSeabed, EMODnet Bathymetry/Human Activities, INFOMAR, CHS and BOEM. Their raw SSS availability and target-disposition fields are unverified [U].
- MDE licence: the terms of use and the "Requirements for providing data to the MDE" document were not retrieved. Email mde@thecrownestate.co.uk.

## 3. Ground-truth gold: sonar contacts later physically verified

### Takeaway
Physically verified contact outcomes exist mainly in four places: (1) offshore-wind UXO target lists with ROV/diver investigation results, often reproduced in archaeology grey literature on the Archaeology Data Service (ADS) and in Scottish marine licence appendices; (2) NOAA DRs and feature disposition; (3) ghost-gear removal programmes (WWF Baltic, Puget Sound/Northwest Straits, Chesapeake ghost-pot programme), where retrieval confirms the target; (4) archaeology wreck records. None is packaged as an ML dataset. The GhostNetZero paper data (239 Baltic and 173 Puget Sound annotated segments) has no public data-availability statement.

### Cited Findings
- GhostNetZero (Microsoft AI for Good, WWF Germany, Accenture, Fenn Enterprises; EcoEvoRxiv preprint): Baltic data from the WWF Ghostnet Program, 2019–2023, Marine Sonic Mark II at 600 kHz, 5–18 m depth, 239 annotated segments. Puget Sound data from 2014 (Fenn Enterprises with Northwest Straits Foundation and Natural Resources Consultants), Sea Scan PC at 600 kHz, to about 36 m, 173 targets. Labels were "manually annotated and verified by marine science experts" (expert annotation, not necessarily diver-confirmed). Splits were 205/34 and 145/28 with no test set. Centroid detection was 0.891–0.929 at a 20 px buffer. State programmes in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern and Schleswig-Holstein since 2022 supply "a growing stream of annotated sonar data". I found **no data-availability statement** in the extracted text [V] — [GhostNetZero preprint](https://ecoevorxiv.org/repository/object/10294/download/19034)
- Stolte et al. 2022 (Frontiers in Marine Science) found sonar to be the most efficient way to locate lost gear in the German Baltic WWF pilot. I did not confirm data availability [S] — [DOAJ record](https://doaj.org/article/10f10ee3fd574ef6b319e17ed8ff635c); [WWF detect ghost nets](https://www.wwf.de/themen-projekte/projektregionen/ostsee/geisternetze/detect-ghost-nets)
- GhostNetBusters (GEOMAR with sonoware GmbH, June 2024 to Jan 2026) uses SSS to detect lost nets — [GEOMAR](https://geomar.de/en/ghostnetbusters); [sonoware](https://sonoware.de/news/2024-08-28_foerderzusage_ghostnetbusters)
- Chesapeake (Virginia) ghost-pot programme (VIMS/VMRC, from 2008): watermen used side-imaging sonar to find and retrieve derelict pots, recovering 9,970 pots, 52 nets and 532 other items. Every retrieval is a physical verification. In a York River pilot, 27 of 33 derelict traps removed (82%) were still functional [S] — [VIMS 2011](https://www.vims.edu/newsandevents/topstories/archives/2011/ghost_pots_2011.php); [VMRC results](https://www.mrc.virginia.gov/news_releases/nr_ghost_pot_program_results.pdf)
- Offshore-wind UXO verification in public grey literature: Wessex Archaeology reports on ADS assess UXO survey results (e.g. Galloper OFTO: all 26 UXO targets archaeologically assessed; Dudgeon 2014: ROV investigation within 150 m of 15 of 67 turbines and along the export cable). The NEMOLink report includes deep-ocean target investigation reports for a UXO anomaly and ROV work on possible aircraft wreckage [S] — [ADS Galloper](https://archaeologydataservice.ac.uk/catalogue/adsdata/arch-425-1/dissemination/pdf/1014026/wessexar1-518213_213954.pdf); [ADS Dudgeon](https://archaeologydataservice.ac.uk/catalogue/adsdata/arch-425-1/dissemination/pdf/wessexar1-343289_1.pdf); [DataCite Dudgeon](https://commons.datacite.org/doi.org/10.5284/1091021); [ADS NEMOLink](https://archaeologydataservice.ac.uk/catalogue/adsdata/arch-425-1/dissemination/pdf/wessexar1-394303_1.pdf)
- Scottish marine licence UXO technical appendices are public PDFs on marine.gov.scot. Industry tenders describe a live "Master Target List" plus boulder target lists (Elia/50Hertz). At Trianel Borkum II, SeaTerra identified 11 major UXO (7 buoyant mines, 2 ground mines, 1 depth charge, 1 bomb) [S] — [marine.gov.scot UXO appendix](https://marine.gov.scot/sites/default/files/technical_appendix_19.2_-_unexploded_ordnance_technical_report.pdf); [offshorewind.biz](https://www.offshorewind.biz/2023/03/07/50hertz-elia-issue-uxo-survey-and-clearance-call/); [offshore-energy.biz](https://www.offshore-energy.biz/seaterra-keeps-trianel-windpark-borkum-ii-uxo-free)
- US ESTCP MM-0324 marine UXO reports (SAIC 2008–09) with target investigations are publicly released via DTIC. Fisheries and Oceans Canada's Navy Island UXO survey final report (2022) includes target selection and investigation sections — [DTIC ADA512824](https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/pdfs/ADA512824.pdf); [CanadaBuys Navy Island](https://canadabuys.canada.ca/documents/pub/att/2022/05/02/c3a6519e02aed53666892bbdbf1a5bc1/appendix_b_-_final_report_navy_island_2022-01-31_r3.01.pdf)
- Kraken Robotics (Darwin, Australia): ROV-based sub-bottom imaging "reduced the master target listing", so each target was resolved — [Kraken case study](https://www.krakenrobotics.com/case-studies/uxo-identification-and-clearance-survey/)

### Inferences
- UXO campaigns are the richest real-world source of contact → outcome pairs (sonar/mag contact → ROV ID → "UXO / debris / boulder / nothing"). They contain many verified false alarms, which is exactly what calibration needs. The tables are in PDFs, though, and the raw SSS behind them sits with developers or survey contractors (Ørsted, RWE, Fugro, Gardline, N-Sea, etc.). Legal access route: request via MDE where the data are "no longer restricted", or sign an NDA data licence with a developer or UXO contractor, offering free reprocessing or a QA audit in return.
- Ghost-gear removal programmes are the most mission-aligned gold: retrieval equals verification. Realistic partners are WWF Germany (GhostNetZero is designed for expert validation and retraining), Northwest Straits Foundation/NRC (Puget Sound), VIMS/VMRC (Chesapeake) and GGGI members. A data-sharing MoU offering NetSight calibration analyses in return is the plausible route.
- The NOAA "Danger to Navigation" and disproval records give verified negatives for wrecks and obstructions at no cost under CC0.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether WWF/GhostNetZero, Stolte et al., or the Puget Sound data can be obtained (no availability statement found). The GGGI data portal, the NOAA Marine Debris Program derelict-trap SSS datasets and the Northwest Straits derelict gear database were not checked [U].
- MDE UXO report availability and licence were not confirmed.

## 4. India: data policies and access routes

### Takeaway
I found no published Indian side-scan dataset. Indian marine geophysical data sit with the Navy NHO, GSI (Marine & Coastal Survey Division), NIOT, NIO and INCOIS. Access is governed by NDSAP (restricted data needs authorisation; agencies keep "negative lists"), INCOIS paid and committee-approved access, and geospatial restrictions. The realistic route is a sponsored academic collaboration (IIT Madras, NIO Goa, NIOT, GSI) rather than purchase.

### Cited Findings
- NDSAP 2012 covers non-sensitive, publicly funded data. Restricted data are accessible "only through authorization", and each agency defines a negative list [S, 2012 presentation] — [NDSAP slides](https://de.slideshare.net/DataPortalIndia/national-data-sharing-and-accessibility-policy-ndsap-2012)
- INCOIS is mandated by MoES to acquire all marine-environment data from national programmes. Standard services are public, customised services for commercial users are paid, and requests from foreign nationals go to a Director-appointed committee. The MoES Earth System Science Data portal (hosted by INCOIS) is the discovery point. IIOE-2 has an open data policy with DOIs for endorsed projects [S] — [CODATA DSJ 2018](https://datascience.codata.org/article/10.5334/dsj-2018-011); [IIOE-2 data policy](https://iioe-2.incois.gov.in/documents/IIOE-2/IIOSC2023/IIOE-2%20Data&InformationPolicyDocument.pdf)
- NHO charts are sold through authorised agents. NHO was working on an ENC marketing strategy per IHO NIOHC national reports (older) [S] — [NIOHC11 India report](https://docs.iho.int/mtg_docs/rhc/NIOHC/NIOHC11/NIOHC11-8A_India.pdf); [NIOHC10 India](https://legacy.iho.int/mtg_docs/rhc/NIOHC/NIOHC10/NIOHC10_National-Report-India.pdf)
- India's 2021 geospatial guidelines liberalised map-making but kept prohibitions on sensitive digitisation and sharing [S] — [TEAL white paper](https://tealindia.github.io/research/TEAL%20White%20Paper%208-%20Geospatial%20Regulations%20White%20Paper.pdf)
- GSI hardware: Kongsberg GeoAcoustics 2094D side-scan for a new GSI survey vessel (2013, ten GSI geologists trained). The RV Samudra Ratnakar carries **SAMS DT-6000 synthetic aperture mapping sonar** (Current Science 2017, GSI M&CSD authors). M&CSD operates in Indian territorial waters and the EEZ — [Kongsberg 2013](https://www.kongsberg.com/maritime/news-and-events/news-archive/2013/kongsberg-geoacoustics-sidescan-sonar-package-for-new-indian-survey-vessel); [Current Science note](https://i-scholar.in/index.php/CURS/article/view/156023/143870)
- CSIR-NIO has published SSS and bathymetric surveys of the central western Indian shelf (papers, not datasets) — [ICAR/NIO record](https://krishi.icar.gov.in/ohs-2.3.1/index.php/record/view/454514)
- The S3Simulator synthetic SSS benchmark (Kamal Basha S and Athira Nambiar) is at github.com/bashakamal/S3Simulator (paper CC BY 4.0, dataset licence not stated). The arXiv page does not list affiliations [V] — [arXiv 2408.12833](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12833)

### Inferences
- Indian real SSS/SAS data exists (GSI SAMS DT-6000, the GSI 2094D, NIOT/NIO cruises), but no public channel for it was found. The practical play is an MoU with a public institute under which data stay on-premise or the NetSight model is run on their data ("model-to-data"). That avoids NDSAP and geospatial export issues.
- Indian commercial ports and dredging/port-survey contractors (IWAI waterway surveys, port trusts) are a non-defence route for SSS and MBES data swaps [inference; not researched].

### Gaps
- Not verified: data.gov.in marine listings, the NIOT data portal, NCCR, Deep Ocean Mission data policy, IWAI hydrographic data, Bhukosh offshore layers, and whether NHO bathymetry is on an NDSAP negative list.
- S3Simulator author affiliation (possibly Indian) was not confirmed.

## 5. Simulators and synthetic data; sim-to-real evidence

### Takeaway
Simulators exist (HoloOcean SSS/MBES/FLS, Gazebo-based S3Simulator, SEE paired sim-real), and diffusion synthesis helps when mixed with real data. The best cited result is a roughly 60% AP gain for mine-like-object detection when DDPM/DDIM synthetic images were added to real ones. Synthetic-only training transfers poorly. A 2026 physics-informed simulator paper adds statistical validation against real data.

### Cited Findings
- Syn2Real (Agrawal et al., arXiv 2410.12953): DDPM/DDIM-generated SSS (vs a DCGAN baseline). Mask-RCNN trained on synthetic plus original data showed about a 60% increase in AP over real-only. The authors say synthetic images help even when not perfectly realistic, partly because of residual noise [S, abstract-level] — [arXiv 2410.12953](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.12953v1)
- HoloOcean added simulated side-scan, single-beam and multibeam sonar (Potokar et al. IROS 2022). HoloOcean 2.0 (2025) adds ROS 2 and HIL comparisons. A 2026 BYU thesis re-implements SSS rendering via CPU ray tracing and GPU — [CMU Potokar22](https://frc.ri.cmu.edu/~kaess/pub/Potokar22iros.html); [arXiv 2511.07687](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07687v1); [BYU ETD 11165](https://scholarsarchive.byu.edu/etd/11165); [HoloOcean docs](https://byu-holoocean.github.io/holoocean-docs/v2.2.2/index.html)
- A 2025 review of ROS-compatible marine simulators (including HoloOcean) evaluates sim-to-real capabilities — [arXiv 2504.06245](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.06245v1)
- S3Simulator: SAM-segmented real objects modelled in SelfCAD and placed in Gazebo, with computational imaging to improve realism — [arXiv 2408.12833](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12833)
- SEE dataset: paired HoloOcean simulations and real BlueView P900, explicitly "to mitigate the gap between simulated and real-world sonar data" — [Gomes survey ref 46](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.03353); [alphaxiv](https://www.alphaxiv.org/overview/2505.15465)
- 2026 "Physics-Informed Simulation Framework for Realistic Sonar Image Generation and Statistical Validation" (arXiv 2605.19712); content not read [S] — [arXiv 2605.19712](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.19712)
- GhostNetZero cross-region results show the domain gap even between real datasets: Baltic-only mIoU fell from 0.740 (Baltic) to 0.547 (Puget Sound). Combined training recovered 0.739/0.685 — [GhostNetZero preprint](https://ecoevorxiv.org/repository/object/10294/download/19034)

### Inferences
- Use synthetic data for pretraining and augmentation and for Pd-vs-range/grazing-angle sweeps where ground truth is exact. Calibrate and validate only on real data with known outcomes, since calibration error does not transfer from simulation.
- A common way to reduce the gap is to insert real-object chips into real backgrounds (S3Simulator-style SAM segmentation), plus diffusion with noise realism. This was not quantified in this session.

### Gaps
- Stonefish, UUV Simulator, Gazebo sonar plugins and "SonarSim" were not researched. No quantitative SSS sim-to-real benchmark beyond the Syn2Real abstract was read.

## 6. Data partnership models, vendor samples and crowdsourced data

### Takeaway
I found no public sample XTF downloads from EdgeTech or Klein. Free raw XTF exists through USGS and MGDS, and Oceanic Imaging Consultants hosts "digital sidescan samples". The scalable non-public routes are UXO/offshore-wind contractor data swaps, ghost-gear NGO MoUs (WWF GhostNetZero, Northwest Straits, VIMS) and survey-firm QA services. Recreational side-imaging (Humminbird/Garmin/Lowrance, Navionics SonarChart) was not verified.

### Cited Findings
- Free raw SSS sources: USGS CMGDS Klein XTF; USGS OFR 2008-1174 Klein 5000/3000 XTF; MGDS EdgeTech XTF (NBP cruises); a Mendeley Klein 3000 XTF set; OIC "digital sidescan samples" pages — [USGS CMGDS](https://cmgds.er.usgs.gov/services/cmgds-dataset.php?id=38005); [USGS OFR 2008-1174](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2008/1174/html/data.html); [MGDS](https://www.marine-geo.org/tools/datasets/6159); [OIC samples](https://oicinc.com/digital-sidescan-samples.html)
- WWF GhostNetZero is built as a human-in-the-loop platform: users upload raw sonar, AI flags segments, experts validate, and feedback retrains models. Accenture notes an air-gapped mode is being considered because of data sensitivity, and that much sonar data "ends up buried in archives" — [GhostNetZero preprint](https://ecoevorxiv.org/repository/object/10294/download/19034); [Accenture case](https://www.accenture.com/pl-en/case-studies/song/wwf-germany-hunts-ghost-nets-using-ai)
- The Chesapeake ghost-pot programme paid watermen to run recreational-grade side-imaging sonar. This precedent shows low-cost side-imaging can produce verified derelict-gear detections (9,970 pots recovered) — [VIMS 2011](https://www.vims.edu/newsandevents/topstories/archives/2011/ghost_pots_2011.php)

### Inferences
- Model 1, data-for-service: free AI QA or second-read of contact lists for survey or UXO firms, in return for anonymised contact chips plus outcome codes. Model 2, NGO MoU: NetSight runs calibration for removal programmes, and retrieval logs become labels. Model 3, the Indian academic route (see Q4). Model 4, a hackathon or benchmark seeded with NOAA CC0 data.

### Gaps
- Not verified: Kongsberg/EdgeTech/Klein/SonarWiz/Hypack sample-data pages, Navionics SonarChart and Humminbird/Garmin data terms, and citizen-science SSS projects.

## 7. Top-10 ranking for calibration and Pd validation (with access difficulty)

### Takeaway
The best sources combine real sensor data with physically resolved contact outcomes, including false alarms. Ranked (difficulty: L = download, M = request/email, H = negotiated licence):

### Cited Findings (basis for each rank)
1. **Offshore-wind UXO target lists with ROV/diver ID results** (UK/DE/NL/Scotland). Verified true and false contacts at scale. Difficulty H (raw SSS with developers/contractors); PDFs on ADS and marine.gov.scot are L — [ADS Galloper](https://archaeologydataservice.ac.uk/catalogue/adsdata/arch-425-1/dissemination/pdf/1014026/wessexar1-518213_213954.pdf); [marine.gov.scot](https://marine.gov.scot/sites/default/files/technical_appendix_19.2_-_unexploded_ordnance_technical_report.pdf)
2. **WWF Germany Baltic ghost-net SSS (GhostNetZero; MV/SH state programmes)**. On-mission, expert-verified with retrieval follow-up. Difficulty H (no public release) — [GhostNetZero](https://ecoevorxiv.org/repository/object/10294/download/19034)
3. **NOAA H-surveys: CC0 SSS mosaics + MBAB backscatter + DR contact disposition + Wrecks/Obstructions DB**. Difficulty L–M (parsing PDFs; raw XTF on request) — [NCEI H13684](https://data.ngdc.noaa.gov/platforms/ocean/nos/coast/H12001-H14000/H13684/); [NOAA W&O](https://fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/39988)
4. **Puget Sound derelict gear SSS (Northwest Straits / NRC / Fenn)**. Retrieved targets. Difficulty H — [GhostNetZero](https://ecoevorxiv.org/repository/object/10294/download/19034)
5. **USGS republished NOAA SSS with contact lists plus raw Klein XTF**. Difficulty L — [USGS OFR 2008-1174 contacts](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2008/1174/html/contacts.html)
6. **DFKI UXO acoustic+optical set (BSD-3)**. Known placed objects with 3D GT, imaging sonar only. Difficulty L — [Zenodo 13778485](https://zenodo.org/records/13778485)
7. **AI4Shipwrecks (known sanctuary wrecks, EdgeTech 2205)**. Real AUV SSS. Difficulty L (licence to confirm) — [arXiv 2401.14546](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.14546v2)
8. **Bath mannequin SSS (CC BY)**. Controlled positives on StarFish SSS, good for Pd-vs-range checks. Difficulty L — [Bath 1467](https://researchdata.bath.ac.uk/1467/)
9. **Chesapeake ghost-pot programme records (VIMS/VMRC)**. Retrieval-verified; sonar imagery availability unknown. Difficulty M–H — [VIMS](https://www.vims.edu/newsandevents/topstories/archives/2011/ghost_pots_2011.php)
10. **SCTD 3.0 / SubPipe / Marine Debris FLS (controlled tank/quarry)**. Large real sets for pretraining and detection; outcome verification limited. Difficulty L (licences to confirm except SubPipe CC BY) — [arXiv 2608.08106](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08106); [Zenodo SubPipe](https://zenodo.org/records/10808161); [arXiv 2503.22880](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.22880v1)

### Inferences
- A practical bootstrap: (a) build a CC0 NOAA contact-outcome table now; (b) add controlled-placement sets for Pd-vs-range curves; (c) pursue one ghost-gear NGO MoU and one UXO contractor swap for gear-specific reliability; (d) pursue an Indian institute MoU for local seabed priors. Calibration requires negatives, so prioritise sources that log disproved or "no contact found" outcomes.
- NetSight could also run its own small known-placement trials (deploy decommissioned nets or targets at known GPS in Indian coastal waters, then survey). This makes ground truth by construction and is the only fully controllable source.

### Gaps
- The ranking is a judgement from the evidence above. The volumes of verified contacts per source (e.g. how many UXO targets per wind farm, or how many WWF retrievals) were not quantified.
